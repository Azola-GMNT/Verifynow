# VerifyNow

## Overview

VerifyNow is an enterprise verification platform that enables organisations to verify individuals, companies, assets, licences, mining rights and compliance information through a single unified platform.

The platform provides a standardised verification workflow regardless of the verification provider or country.

---

# Vision

To become Africa's leading verification platform by providing secure, trusted and scalable verification services across governments, financial institutions, mining, healthcare, education and private organisations.

---

# Mission

Provide businesses with one platform to perform trusted verification services while integrating seamlessly with government agencies, commercial verification providers and international compliance systems.
