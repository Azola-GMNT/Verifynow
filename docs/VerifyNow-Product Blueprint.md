# VerifyNow Platform Architecture

Version: 1.0
Last Updated: July 2026

---

# 1. Product Vision

VerifyNow is a pan-African enterprise verification platform that enables organisations to verify individuals, organisations, licences, permits and compliance information through a single trusted platform.

Our goal is to eliminate fragmented verification processes by providing one secure interface that connects to multiple verification providers across Africa and internationally.

---

# 2. Core Principles

Every feature built for VerifyNow must satisfy at least one of these goals:

• Increase trust
• Reduce verification time
• Improve compliance
• Standardise verification results
• Improve operational efficiency

---

# 3. Product Philosophy

VerifyNow consists of three major capabilities.

## Operations

Running and managing verifications.

## Due Diligence

Reviewing verification results and assessing risk.

## Intelligence

Analytics, trends and reporting.
